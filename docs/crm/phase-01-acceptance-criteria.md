## 1. Overview & Verification Strategy

This document establishes the **79 testable, deterministic acceptance criteria** required for all future implementation phases of the Lead & CRM domain (Phases 02–06):
- **Public Lead Ingestion (`AC-ING-01` to `AC-ING-20`):** 20 criteria
- **Lead Lifecycle & Transitions (`AC-LIF-01` to `AC-LIF-09`):** 9 criteria
- **Sales Activities & Scheduling (`AC-ACT-01` to `AC-ACT-04`):** 4 criteria
- **Multi-Domain Conversion Orchestration (`AC-CON-01` to `AC-CON-24`):** 24 criteria
- **Security, RBAC & Tenant Isolation (`AC-SEC-01` to `AC-SEC-17`):** 17 criteria
- **Staff Workbench UI & Reporting (`AC-UI-01` to `AC-UI-05`):** 5 criteria  
*(Total: 20 + 9 + 4 + 24 + 17 + 5 = 79 verified criteria. This corrects the historical typographical mention of "56" in earlier review summaries).*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Specification vs Execution Status Disclaimer:**  
> All 79 criteria defined in this matrix are **SPECIFIED REQUIREMENTS FOR FUTURE IMPLEMENTATION**. None of these criteria are claimed as currently executed or passing, as application code, Prisma schema models, and database migrations remain strictly unauthorized during Phase 01.

Following OOS quality engineering standards, verification spans four testing tiers:
1. **Unit Tests (`tests/unit/crm/`)**: Schema validation, lifecycle transitions, qualification logic, default-deny authorization, and rate-limiting buckets.
2. **Integration Tests (`tests/integration/crm/`)**: Database transactions, PostgreSQL RLS isolation, audit logging, saga failure rollbacks, and stable idempotency.
3. **Cross-Domain Golden Path (`tests/integration/crm-cross-domain-golden-path.test.ts`)**: End-to-end flow from public intake $\rightarrow$ qualification $\rightarrow$ idempotent conversion saga $\rightarrow$ Candidate Core onboarding and CBOS billing setup.
4. **Browser Verification (Subagent E2E)**: Staff Workbench UI testing across desktop (1440px) and mobile (390px) viewports.

Empty query results fail closed safely with structured domain errors. | Automated Security & Unit Test | Specified (Pending Phase 03) |
| **AC-SEC-17** | Global Lock Ordering Adherence Across Administrative Transactions | All administrative codepaths affecting lead ownership (e.g. employee deactivation, team reassignment) and transactional lead operations acquire locks strictly in global canonical order: `Memberships` $\rightarrow$ `Leads` $\rightarrow$ `LeadActivities` $\rightarrow$ `Designations`. No codepath performs lock escalation from `FOR SHARE` to `FOR UPDATE` within the same transaction. Concurrent execution of lead assignment and staff deactivation is guaranteed deadlock-free under PostgreSQL lock hierarchy tests. | Automated Concurrency Test | Specified (Pending Phase 03) |
