## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 8D executes a rigorous, evidence-based forensic product and business value audit across the complete Operations Operating System (OOS) following the successful freezing of the **Interview Operating System (IOS, Phases 8A–8C.5)**.

The core product principle of OOS remains our guiding compass:
> *"People shouldn't manage the business around the system. The system should manage the business around the people."*

### Key Forensic Findings:
1. **Current Implemented Authority:** OOS is now 100% operationally authoritative from Candidate Onboarding through Career Evidence, Match Recommendation, Application Preparation, QA Review, External Submission, Outcome Logging, and the entire multi-round Interview Lifecycle (Screening, Technical, Management, Debriefs, and Concluded Outcomes).
2. **The New Critical Breakpoint:** When an interview concludes with an outcome of `OFFER_RECEIVED` (or advances to final negotiations), **OOS instantly loses operational authority**.
3. **The Post-Interview Operational Black Hole:** Everything related to compensation packages, stock/equity structures, decision deadlines, counter-offers, negotiation strategy, offer acceptance/decline decisions, official placement confirmation, start dates, and probationary milestones currently occurs **100% off-platform** in private email threads, spreadsheets, phone calls, and manual notes.
4. **Strategic Determination:** The single highest-value, revenue-critical, and operationally necessary next domain is the **Offer & Placement Operating System (OPOS)**.

## 4. Current Authority Boundaries

1. **Application Boundary:** Authoritative from discovery through submission, QA, and high-level outcome recording. Does not model downstream hiring compensation.
2. **Outcome Ledger Boundary:** Records raw timestamps and historical events (`OFFER_RECEIVED`). Does not manage offer terms, revisions, or contracts.
3. **Interview OS Boundary:** Manages round logistics, preparation, and debriefing. Concludes with `OFFER_RECEIVED`, but hands off all subsequent operational workflow to the void.
4. **Offer & Placement Boundary (Current State: Non-Existent):** No data models, no service logic, no state machines, no UI components, and zero audit tracking exist for offer terms, negotiations, or placements.
5. **Billing Boundary:** No billing engine exists; billing cannot be linked to successful placements without an authoritative placement record.

---

## 5. Post-Interview Gap

When a candidate receives an offer after a final interview round:
- **No Structured Compensation Ledger:** OOS cannot record base salary, annual bonus target, sign-on bonus, equity units/vesting schedule, relocation stipends, or healthcare benefits.
- **No Expiration / Deadline Management:** Exploding offers with 48–72 hour deadlines are untracked, creating acute operational risk of offer forfeiture.
- **No Counter-Offer / Negotiation Tracking:** The iterative back-and-forth between candidate, career specialist, and hiring manager happens in unrecorded chat channels.
- **No Multi-Offer Comparative Decision Matrix:** Candidates receiving competing offers have no comparative evaluation workspace within their portal.
- **No Official Placement Transition:** The platform cannot formally transition an active candidate from "Job Seeker" to "Successfully Placed", preventing accurate cohort metrics, placement fee realization, and success reporting.
